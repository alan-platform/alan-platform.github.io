In Alan, data models drive everything.
Its unique application modeling language lets you write one model that generates both the database design and the user interface.
That makes it a fast way to create an application and to iterate on it, without sacrificing data correctness.
A dedicated team has developed Alan continuously since 2011, and it has been used to build powerful yet agile systems for multinationals: manufacturing execution systems (MES), ERP modules and specialized custom applications.
{.lead}

## Features

The platform has more features than we have been able to document. These are some of them.

<div class="features">
<div class="feature">
<h3>Bug-free by design</h3>
<p>Applications are free of bugs by design, so no unit testing is required.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature">
<h3>One application language</h3>
<p>Model data, calculations and transformations, numerical types, authorization, procedures and workflow in a single language.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature">
<h3>Windows, Linux and macOS</h3>
<p>Develop on any of the three, with our tooling and extensions for several code editors.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature">
<h3>Generated and custom GUIs</h3>
<p>The generated GUI gives access to all functionality and is easily customized with annotations on the model. A custom GUI in Alan does something very specific, without losing Alan's full error checking.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature">
<h3>Supported by every GUI</h3>
<p>Authentication, internationalization, live data and independent platform updates.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature">
<h3>System interfaces</h3>
<p>Our system interface language lets you describe a system interface as one integrated whole, and map it safely to an application model, in both directions.</p>
</div>
</div>

## Kjerner

Kjerner took over the development of Alan from M&#8209;industries at the beginning of 2019.
M&#8209;industries built Alan to help international businesses such as Tata Steel and Hydro improve their data quality and upgrade their legacy systems.
Kjerner runs fully on Alan itself as well: for accounting, planning and more.
We are based near the campus of Delft University of Technology.
